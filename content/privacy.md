# Website Privacy

This page describes data handling on the OpenLake website. For questions, contact [openlake@iitbhilai.ac.in](mailto:openlake@iitbhilai.ac.in).

## Newsletter

If you submit your email address through the newsletter form, the website sends it to Brevo, our email service provider, to request a confirmation email. You are added to the newsletter list after you confirm through that email. We use the address to send OpenLake newsletters. You can unsubscribe using the link in a newsletter email or contact us at the address above.

The site does not display subscriber addresses publicly. Newsletter signup is unavailable when the email service is not configured.

## Site analytics and other services

The website uses Plausible and Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights to understand visits and performance. The "Ask OpenLake" widget loads from an external service; if you use it, your message is sent to that service. Links to other websites follow those websites' own privacy practices.

For event participation and community behavior, see the [Safeguarding Policy](/safeguarding).
